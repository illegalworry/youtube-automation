# Roblox 3D Studio

**Voiceover → fully animated Roblox-style 3D video.** Claude writes the video as code: a scene/shot script plus small JS files. A Three.js engine animates it, headless Chromium renders it frame by frame, and ffmpeg muxes it with the voice, generated music and automatic sound effects.

*Русский:* скилл превращает озвучку (и сценарий) в полноценный 3D-мультфильм в стиле Roblox. Он даёт:
- блочных персонажей с одеждой, причёсками, шапками, аксессуарами и лицами-эмоциями;
- 60+ анимаций: ходьба, бег, прыжки, драки, падения, жесты, танцы;
- взаимодействие нескольких персонажей, автоматические реакции на удары и липсинк по озвучке;
- кинокамеру (наезды, облёты, слежение, OTS, тряска) с автоматическим обходом препятствий;
- локации, свет, тени, частицы, авто-SFX, музыку и готовый MP4.

Нейросети для картинок не используются, поэтому персонажи одинаковые во всех кадрах.

## What you can say
- "Make a Roblox 3D video from this voiceover" / «Сделай 3D-видео в стиле Роблокс по этой озвучке» runs the whole pipeline (`make-3d-video`).
- `/new-3d-video`: set up the project, timestamp the voiceover, break the script into scenes and shots, and design the cast and locations.
- `/render-3d-video`: build and QC the scenes (contact sheets), preview, and run the final render.
- `/sound-3d-video`: auto SFX on the animation, music moods, and the final mix.

## How it works
- **Scenes** are continuous action in one location. Actors carry timed acts (`walk`, `run`, `jump`, `punch`, `knockback`, `wave`, `dance`, `sit`, `throw`, `hug`, …) anchored to seconds or to spoken words.
- **Shots** are camera cuts over a scene (size, move, target, transition, overlay text), so continuity between angles is automatic.
- The engine solves the details:
  - foot grounding;
  - stride-matched walk cycles;
  - clip blending and smooth turns;
  - hit reactions with flashes, debris and camera shake;
  - landing dust;
  - blinking and lip-sync;
  - item hand-overs and throws;
  - occlusion-aware camera placement.

## Inputs and options
- Voiceover (any audio format); script optional (plain or timestamped SRT/VTT/JSON/TXT); Russian and other languages work, including Cyrillic titles.
- Aspect: 16:9 or 9:16.
- Resolution: 720p–4K at 24 fps.
- Looks: `bright`, `cinematic`, `cartoon`, `horror`, `retro`.
- Music: generated by mood, your own track, or none.

## Setup (automatic on first use)
The kit is copied to `roblox-3d-studio/` in your workspace, and then these are installed:
- ffmpeg;
- Node packages (Three.js, Playwright, fonts);
- Chromium (unless one is preinstalled);
- numpy, scipy and faster-whisper.

No API keys. No GPU required (SwiftShader); a GPU makes rendering much faster.

## Render times (CPU only)
About 1.7 frames/s at 720p on a 4-thread cloud machine, so ~14 min per minute of video. 1080p takes ~2× longer. Use `--quality draft` for fast previews.

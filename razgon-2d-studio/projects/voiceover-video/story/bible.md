# What If You Had a Perfect Memory? — story bible

**Logline.** Max ("you", 17) suddenly remembers everything. It's a party trick (raincoats, timetables, 52 cards), then a burden (grudges, sleepless replays, pain that never fades) — until a scammer, Mr. Black, targets Grandpa Leo at the bank. Max can't share his memory as proof, so he uses the one thing they both keep: Leo's secret knock. Ends on a voice Max can't remember, then a "how much is real?" science segment.

**Tone.** Comedy/wonder (plus/minus montage) → melancholy (forgetting is cleanup) → warmth (Leo) → suspense (bank) → bittersweet → mystery tease → blueprint facts. Second person: "you" = Max.

# What If You Could Think 10x Faster? — story bible

**Logline.** One morning a teenager, Max ("you"), wakes up with a brain running ten times faster than everyone else's. It is a superpower and a slow curse at the same time: a body that can't keep up, hunger, boredom, a stranger who wants to own him. At his little sister Mia's talent show, the speed lets him save her with one silly word — "Pancake!" — at the price of his secret.

**Tone.** Wonder + comedy for the first two acts (plus/minus rhythm), creeping dread (the clip, Mr. Black), suspense for the theater climax, warmth for the ending, a cold sci-fi tease at the end (Nova). Second person: "you" = Max.

## Motifs
- **10x badge** in the corner whenever we're "inside" Max's time (freeze shots), cool tint + hanging particles.
- **PLUS / MINUS** slam cards (green / coral) every time the narrator says "Plus." / "Minus."
- **Clock** — the wall clock that ticks slower; opens the film (7:15) and closes it.
- **Spark** — a small gold spark for dopamine/surprise; it never lights… until Mia's pancake joke.
- **Gray** — Mr. Black's gray coat and gray car are the only gray in the film.

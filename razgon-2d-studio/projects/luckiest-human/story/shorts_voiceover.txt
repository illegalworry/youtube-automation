Every coin lands your way. Every door opens. And you have nine days before it breaks you.
Tonight, your luck becomes perfect.
Day one: every light is green, and your forgotten lottery ticket wins. One in 292 million.
Day two: you bet everything on red at the casino. Red. Again. Red.
Day three: you cross a busy road without looking. The truck stops an inch away.
You feel nothing. No fear. No thrill.
Day five: your team scores in the last second. The stadium explodes. You just sit there.
Day seven: you overhear your friend. "He didn't do anything. Things just work out for him."
Day eight: you find the one thing luck can't fix. A person.
You ask a girl to dinner. Your voice shakes.
She says no.
And it hurts. Sharp, real. It's the best you've felt in nine days.
Next morning, the luck is gone. You miss your bus. And you laugh.
Because the luckiest people aren't the ones who never lose.
They're the ones who still get to find out.

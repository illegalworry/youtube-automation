Look at the person closest to you. Imagine a number floating above their head: the exact day they will die. Now imagine everyone on Earth sees it tomorrow. Everyone, except you. You can't see your own.
In Tokyo, a patient dies exactly on his date. Within a week, the economy collapses. Your date becomes your credit score.
People test it. They jump off bridges. They survive. Every single time.
Then, six months in, a scientist in Seoul puts millions of dates on one graph. And one day shoots straight up off the chart.
Two billion people. Same date. September 9th, 2031.
But then a seven-year-old girl in Portugal notices something impossible. One number just moved.
What did she do? The answer is not what you think.

# What If You Used 100% of Your Brain? — story bible

**Logline.** Max ("you") takes a free trial of Lumen Labs' headband from the smooth Mr. Black and for three weeks his brain runs at 100%: perfect memory, instant answers, chess, piano, maths proofs. The price is noise, hunger, sleeplessness, boredom and loneliness — and when his best friend Leo collapses on the launch stage under a falling screen, Max spends the band's one-use emergency "Burst" to save him, then tells the truth on a live stream. He ends normal, slower and happy… until a scan shows one bright spot still awake, and an unknown number texts: "The band was only the beginning."

**Tone.** Wonder + comedy (days 1–15, plus/minus rhythm), creeping cost (noise, hunger, boredom, Dr. Lee), dread (the spreadsheet), suspense (the launch), warmth (hospital), sci-fi tease (coda). Second person: "you" = Max.

## Motifs
- **The headband** — cyan = on, glowing hotter (orange) as the cost grows, gray when dead. The tiny red switch is introduced at 1:37 and paid off at 14:54.
- **PLUS / MINUS** slam cards whenever the narrator says "Plus." / "Minus."; **DAY n** badge in the corner for the montage.
- **The secret knock** (two fast, one slow) — window at 10:27, bed rail at 18:45.
- **The spark** — dopamine, absent all film, lights up at 18:37 when Max laughs on time.
- **Gray** — Mr. Black's coat; desaturated grade on "inside, it's quiet" moments.

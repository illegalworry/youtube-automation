# What If You Could Win Every Argument? — story bible

**Logline.** Max ("you") hears half a sentence and sees every flaw. He wins everything — broccoli, bus timetables, phone bills, debate club, live TV, the city council — and every win leaves the other person a little smaller. When a fake scout, Mr. Black, comes for his best friend Leo's dream and his dad's savings, Max's perfect arguments fail: Leo doesn't need him to be right. Max wins by saying the loser's line on purpose — "You win. I hate it." — and finally learns to lose.

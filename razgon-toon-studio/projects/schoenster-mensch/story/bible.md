# Was wäre, wenn du der schönste Mensch der Welt wärst?

**Language:** German narration (ElevenLabs), all on-screen text in German. 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps, style `toon`. Voiceover 398.55 s → video 400.05 s.

## Logline and tone
"You" become the most beautiful person in the world: every door opens, yet nobody tells you the truth anymore. The one friend who still says "Du siehst furchtbar aus" gets framed by a powerful agent, and you have to choose between a €10M contract and the truth.
Tone: playful wonder (acts 1–3) → loneliness (act 3 end) → warmth (act 4) → suspense/drama (act 5) → calm facts + cliffhanger (act 6).
**"Du" = SAM** (the narrator calls them Sam once at 3:47). Second-person shots show Sam.

## Motifs
- **Knock code** `• • • —` (dreimal kurz, einmal lang): drawn as dots + dash graphic each time Jonas knocks; returns as the proof on the door camera.
- **"Du siehst furchtbar aus"** in a handwritten `toonBubble` from Jonas (3×: morning, door camera, final scene).
- **Doors** (`tueren`): "Dein Gesicht öffnet jede Tür" — hallway of doors opening by themselves; returns in the climax.
- **PLUS / MINUS stickers** (green `+` / red `−`) for every Plus/Minus line.
- **Counters** for every number: 0,1 s, 2006, 4 Fehler, 100.000 / 1.000.000 Likes, 30 Handys, 3 Std, 200 / 2000, 20 m, 30 s, 15×/min, 100 Länder, 365 Tage / 0 × Nein, 10 Mio €, 50.000 €, 20 Mio Zuschauer, 60 s, 70 bpm, 23:41, 06:00, 1972, 1990.

# What If You Turned Invisible? — story bible

**Logline.** One ordinary person turns invisible at 7:12 AM and lives the day hour by hour under real physics. The power falls apart (blind, naked, freezing, tracked, terrifying), and the real twist is that nobody notices they are gone.
**Tone.** Curious wonder → comedy of errors → loneliness → quiet hope. "You" = the viewer.
**Narration.** English voiceover = part 1 (6 s hook) + part 2 (11:05), joined with a 0.35 s gap. 671 s total.
